# Beauty Studio Customer Segmentation & Loyalty Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on customer segmentation and loyalty tracking for a beauty salon operations dataset. The goal is to analyze customer purchase frequency, total spending, and visit patterns to group clients into distinct loyalty tiers and enable data-driven retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Problems & Objectives
* **Customer Segmentation:** Group clients based on spending behavior, visit frequency, and engagement history using RFM & Loyalty Tier logic.
* **Revenue Drivers:** Identify key customer segments generating the highest share of total revenue (evaluating dataset performance up to 4.7M+ total revenue).
* **Retention Strategy:** Provide actionable insights for marketing and service management to increase repeat visits and customer lifetime value.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Google Sheets:** Data cleaning, advanced formulas, cohort modeling, and custom metrics calculation.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, DAX measures, customer segmentation visual reports.
* **Beauty Salon CRM / POS Data:** Raw transactional export analysis, customer visit history, and sales tracking.

## 📊 Key Findings & Business Insights
* **Segment Distribution:** Identified top-tier loyal clients contributing a major portion of total revenue versus one-time visitors.
* **Loyalty Tiers:** Categorized clients into actionable segments to tailor personalized promotional offers and follow-up reminders.
* **Cross-Platform Consistency:** Verified matching aggregate metrics and calculated totals across Google Sheets and Power BI visual dashboards.
